Press Start captions during a lecture or meeting. What is said appears here, a few seconds behind. Or drop a recording (.wav, .mp3, .m4a) on the right. Questions and diagrams can use these captions.

Ask & draw

How it works
Attach a whiteboard photo, a slide, a syllabus (PDF, .txt) or a recording, then ask about it. With nothing attached, questions are answered from the captions. Who's doing what draws the decisions, tasks and deadlines in them as a graph.
Draw a diagram turns a request ("a flowchart of the late-day policy", "a timeline of who does what") into a diagram you can edit, drawn from the captions or the attached photo.
