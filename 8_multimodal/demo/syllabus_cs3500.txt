CS 3500: Object-Oriented Design -- Fall 2026 Syllabus

Instructor: Prof. Dana Whitfield
Office hours: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 2:00-3:30 PM, West Village H 210.
Teaching assistants hold office hours Monday to Friday; the schedule is on the course website.
Lectures: Monday and Wednesday, 10:30-11:50 AM, Richards Hall 300.

Accessibility: Students who need accommodations (for example live captioning, a note-taker, or extra exam time)
should register with the Disability Resource Center and send the instructor their accommodation letter in the
first two weeks of the semester. All lecture videos are posted with captions within 48 hours.

Course description: This course teaches how to design and build programs that are easy to change. Topics
include interfaces, abstract classes, inheritance versus composition, the model-view-controller pattern,
design patterns such as Strategy, Observer and Visitor, and testing with JUnit.

Grading

Homework assignments (8 total): 40 percent
Midterm exam: 20 percent
Final group project: 30 percent
Lecture participation quizzes: 10 percent

Late policy: Each student has 3 late days for the whole semester. A late day extends a homework deadline by
24 hours. Late days cannot be used for the final project. After the late days are used up, late homework
loses 10 percent per day and is not accepted after 3 days.

Collaboration: You may discuss ideas with classmates, but all code you submit must be written by you.
Using AI coding assistants is allowed for homework only if you cite them in your README.

Schedule

Week 1-2: Interfaces and abstract classes. Homework 1 due Friday September 18 at 11:59 PM.
Week 3-4: Inheritance versus composition. Homework 2 due Friday October 2.
Week 5-6: Model-view-controller. Homework 3 due Friday October 16.

Week 8-10: Design patterns: Strategy, Observer, Visitor. Homeworks 4 to 6.
Week 11-13: Final group project, teams of 3. Project proposal due November 13.
Week 14: Project presentations on December 2 and December 7.
The final project code is due December 9 at 11:59 PM. There is no final exam.
